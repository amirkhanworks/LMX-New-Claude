Case studies

A few of the systems we’ve engineered end to end, from schematic to deployment.

Client
SurgIQ · Racing Camel Industry
Challenge
Track real-time vitals of racing camels: ECG, SpO₂, gait, GNSS speed, body temperature, RFID, live over LTE.
What we built
Fully custom wearable with purpose-designed PCB, embedded firmware, cellular LTE, and a live trackside dashboard.
Outcome
System deployed and operational in the field. Trainers track vitals and identity in real time during race day.
Tech spec
Custom 4-layer PCB (KiCad) · ESP32-S3 · ECG/PPG/IMU/GNSS/SpO₂/RFID · Cellular LTE / MQTT

Challenge
Give farmers real-time visibility into soil moisture, nutrient levels (NPK), and pH, without expensive infrastructure or constant manual sampling.
What we built
Wireless sensor network with solar-powered field nodes transmitting data over LoRa to a local gateway. A live dashboard shows actionable soil health insights and supports precision irrigation decisions.
Tech spec
3 soil parameters live: moisture, NPK, pH · Solar-powered, zero wiring in the field · LoRa connectivity · ESP32

Client
US-based pet health client
Challenge
Bring real-time biometric tracking, the same approach behind CamelX, to a wearable built for dogs.
Status
Currently in active development, extending our wearable biometric-tracking platform to canine health monitoring. Builds on the CamelX wearable platform.
