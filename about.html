Our story

Luminox began with Fahad Khan, an electrical engineer who wanted to bring real smart home automation to Indian homes, without the cost and hassle of rewiring a house from scratch. Saad Khan joined early to shape the product’s mechanical design. Amir Khan came on board to lead technology and business. Today, the three of them are focused on scaling both the smart home product and Luminox’s growing engineering services work.

Founded 2020 · Patent-pending · 100+ on the Mumbai waiting list · Soft launching soon
